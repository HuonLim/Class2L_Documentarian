SCENE 1 2 min 51 sec (18/8 Aug)
Location: Private event space.
Context: Mark Zuckerberg is hosting a party to celebrate the success of Facebook.
MLS Cues: Project Scene1: Party Background + facebook logo banner (3x: Left, Middle, Right in audience stand). NO CLASS LIGHTS FOR SCENE 1-2.
Plebeians: Make noise and chatter. When Mark comes in, start clapping.
START MUSIC -> MUSIC off
MARK Welcome, everyone. (Sweep both hands over the crowd)
Floodlight white -> Spotlight on Mark
MARK Two years ago, I sat in a dormitory and wrote code while everyone else went out. I knew what this could be. (Point to table with intensity) Nobody in that building did. (Slow down on nobody, emphasise it)
Brighter spotlight -> Spotlight dies back down to OG
MARK Before I continue, there are a few people I want to thank. Firstly, Eduardo.
Shine spotlight on Eduardo. Then back to Mark quickly.
MARK Facebook would not be what it is today without him. (Points to Eduardo) He believed in it from the very start, stayed by my side through thick and thin, (one palm low, one palm high) and poured in his very best before it ever went viral. Thank you for supporting me in my journey.
Play applause sound.
[Mark walks over to Eduardo, Eduardo stands up with a smile, shakes mark hands. Long, warm handshake to signify trust]
MARK And Sean.
Change spotlight to be on Sean.
MARK Sean is the reason this company is the size it is. He is also the most reliable right-hand man I know. Thank you.
[He walks to Sean to shake hands]
SEAN When you ask me to do something, it will be done.
[Sean is talking to Mark, not the people whilst they shake hands. Firm handshake to show loyalty]
Play applause sound. Clap after Sean’s line only. Change spotlight back to Mark.
MARK Tonight is about what can happen when people believe in an idea. When people put their blood, sweat and tears into a vision. Every big idea started small, (open arms out first, then show squeeze) and Facebook is proof of that. So thank you for being here. Thank you for believing in Facebook. We are just getting started.
(Brighter spotlight again)
[Pause]
MARK Oh and I also want thank... Tyler. (Mark raise his hand up and hold it there to show forgetting of names) And, uhh…
turn off WB
[Looks down at his script] (have a pause here to show forget)
MARK Cameron. Yes. Cameron. They helped. A bit. At the start. (Can have both twins show physical reaction to Mark speech)
[Mark leaves]
MUSIC same as party, softer. Change classroom light to R2 ONLY. Turn off lighting for left and middle. Spotlight on twins. Project dimmer party background.
Cassius soliloquy
TYLER He went from being our programmer to CEO, while we’re now the stepping stones for his success.
CAMERON Mark is like a vampire. He steals what he doesn’t have from us.
TYLER If Mark is a vampire, then we must kill! Kill this bloodsucker, Mark Zuckerberg, before he bleeds others dry. (Stare straight in front to show cold conviction)
Turn off R2, On R3 + try and make spotlights lower
CAMERON We need somebody to help us. (Step nearer to Tyler to reason with him) We can’t do anything to Mark right now. (Put arm over Tyler) We aren’t even a part of Facebook, or at least they don’t think we are.
TYLER You’re right. But I know somebody who can help us. Eduardo. He’s a part of Facebook, and he is like a tailor. We can look imperfect and vulgar, but with him (gesture outwards), we would look tidy and generous. (Slow down here, and stare straight ahead for dramatic impact)
Weak spotlight on Eduardo
CAMERON Yes, this is the way.
turn off all classroom lighting

SCENE 2 5 mins 35 sec (8:28 total)
Location: Same as Scene 1, after the party.
Context: The room is a mess. Almost everyone has left. Only a few dim lights are on.
FLOODLIGHTS: Warm lights change to warm + white lights (loaned from Lit teachers). NO CLASS LIGHTS FOR SCENE 1-2. PREPARE TO SHINE LIGHT ON CHRIS AND MAKE SHADOW (WHOEVER'S IN CHARGE OF LIGHTS RUN BEHIND MARK AND MAKE HIS SHADOW GO TOWARDS STAGE). SPOTLIGHT on Mark, follow Eduardo ONLY briefly. MUSIC make a bit softer.
MARK I hope you enjoyed your time here, Eduardo.
EDUARDO I've had a great time, Mark. The party was great. (Bends down to talk to Mark to show Eduardo should not be at a higher level)
MARK Good. I'll head off, then.
(He stops.) (Put hand over Eduardo, and pretend to whisper to him)
MARK Be wary of the twins in the corner over there. (Gestures.) They have a hungry look in their eyes. They've been watching me all night, but they don't even work here.
EDUARDO I'm sure it's nothing. (Shakes head and waves hand to show he doesn’t think much of it) See you tomorrow. (Sits down)
TYLER (walking towards Eduardo, Cameron a few steps behind) Hello, old friend. Heard about your recent success with Facebook. Congratulations.
EDUARDO Thank you.
Floodlights Warm
TYLER I still remember Harvard when we were all good friends. (Both sit down beside Eduardo to show they are “inferior”)
EDUARDO Yeah. Those were the good old days… Anyway — what is it? Why did you stay until everyone had gone?
CAMERON We want to talk about Mark. You know how Facebook started; we all do. It started with our platform, HarvardConnection. (taking out his phone) This is an email from Mark. Sent to us during the six weeks we were paying him to build our platform.
Turn on R3 and turn off R2 of classroom lights ONLY. SHINE SPOTLIGHT ON EDUARDO. Project cropped email.
(He shows a cropped image of the email. It is projected on the screen.)
EDUARDO (show visible shock, opens mouth to try to argue, then Tyler interrupts) He…
TYLER He said he was busy, but not with what. (Now Tyler stands up. Emphasise "not with what")
EDUARDO (looking closer) This is cropped. (Sound confused, visible disbelief)
CAMERON The rest of it is an excuse. Cropped or not, the message doesn't change.
EDUARDO Look - whatever happened between you and Mark, I'm sorry for it. But that's your case, not mine. (Speak with conviction to show he does not want to take part)
Project blank screen, email put away. Spotlight on Eduardo.
TYLER Thirty-four percent. That's what you held.
EDUARDO That's what I hold--
CAMERON Held. (slight pause) There was a share reissue in April. Everyone's stake was diluted and then topped back up. Everyone's except yours. (Speak with cold tone, emphasise except yours)
have light on Eduardo dim
EDUARDO (taking the phone now) (Stand up to show frustration) That's not true! I'd know. I sign everything that comes through.
TYLER You do. You signed this one too. (Matter-of-fact tone)
EDUARDO (quietly) Under one percent. (Voice fade here)
FLOODLIGHTS: WARM. PREPARE TO SHINE LIGHT ON CHRIS AND MAKE SHADOW TOWARDS STAGE.
TYLER (Tyler & Cameron circle around Eduardo slowly) Someone’s being made CEO next week. Did he tell you that? He wants to shrink you to an ant while he grows into a giant.
Shine torch behind Mark’s head, project shadow onto wall, make it grow.
TYLER If I were you, I would rather leave than to be part of something in which only one man benefits. (Use a finger to gesture one)
CAMERON He called it his company.
TYLER His journey.
CAMERON His money.
TYLER As if none of us were ever in the room. (Gesture 1, 2, 3 on fingers; sweep over audience)
(Pause)
CAMERON Isn't it strange, how the people who write his story disappears from it? (Pause)
TYLER We're not asking you to lie. We're just asking what you already know.
(A long pause.) (Eduardo looks down, reflecting)
EDUARDO I'll think about it. (Slumps into chair)
MUSIC ominous music louder. MING ZE SHINE GREGORY. Close ALL classroom lighting. Floodlights off. DIM FLASHLIGHT ON EDUARDO.
(Exeunt CAMERON and TYLER. EDUARDO remains, holding his phone.)

Project 4th notification. BRIGHTER FLASHLIGHT ON EDUARDO (DO NOT POINT TO DINGNAN).
@Justice4Harvard ding nan: Facebook did not outgrow Harvard Connection. Facebook stole from it.
Floodlights WARM. ALL FLASHLIGHTS on EDUARDO.
EDUARDO (Eduardo soliloquy 1) Even the public is calling him a monster. He is not just driven; he is spinning out of control. If nobody forces him to look in the mirror, he may destroy everyone around him just to keep his throne.
Project blank
EDUARDO I think of Mark as a lion cub. We must kill him while he is still not much of a threat and rather harmless.
Project calling. TURN ON CLASSROOM R3. (Dials. Rings on speaker: "TYLER — calling…"). MUSIC softer. Spotlight on Cameron. Floodlights OFF. BRIGHTER FLASHLIGHT in Eduardo.
CAMERON (voice; immediate) Hello, Eduardo, have you decided yet?
EDUARDO I have. I will join your lawsuit.
CAMERON I am glad that we were able to fire up such passion in you.
EDUARDO Make no mistake --- I'm not doing this for you.
CAMERON It would make sense that we sue Sean with Mark, right? He might inherit Mark's position.
EDUARDO No, Tyler, we must not be excessive. Mark is the engine, Sean is the car. Without Mark, Sean is NOTHING. If we sue Sean as well, it will be overkill, and we will be seen as savage.

SCENE 3 5 mins 35 sec (8:28 total)
Location: Traditional courtroom.
Context: Eduardo has joined the Winklevoss case against Mark and is going to testify against him.
MUSIC gradually off. Turn on ALL class lights -> Only WB and R1 CLASSROOM LIGHT ON. FLOODLIGHTS: Bright white lights x2 under witness stand. Spotlight on Witness stand & speaker.
JUDGE (slams gavel) Winklevoss brothers, you have accused Mark Zuckerberg of stealing your idea of making a social network.
CAMERON (stands up) That is true. We hired Mark as our programmer, but instead he (points to Mark angrily) stole our idea while claiming that he was busy. Here is evidence.
[Cameron passes cropped email image to judge]
Project email. Spotlight on Mark. Floodlights WHITE.
MARK (scoffs) That email is clearly cropped. I was studying for midterms, a much better spend of my time than studying that useless dating website.
Project blank
MARK I am like the sun, and you the Earth. You can believe that the sun revolves around Earth, but it is obvious that it is you who are inferior. In this world, I do not know but one who shines as bright as the sun. And that man is me. I have shown it, by building Facebook from the ground up.
TYLER Eduardo will back us up.
MARK (Scoffs) I don’t know what you are on. He has been loyal to me ever since Facebook started. I have given him more than enough. He is completely satisfied with his role in Facebook.
TYLER Is he? (A brief smile)
MARK Do I have to say it twice? We met in Harvard University, even before Facebook. We are the limbs of the same body. Branches of the same tree. Facebook brought us closer together, if anything.
Knock on door. [Eduardo walks in]
Switch off WB AND R1 lights. SWITCH ON R3 CLASSROOM LIGHTS ONLY.
MARK (barely audible) You too, Eduardo? (devastated)
TURN OFF ALL CLASS LIGHTS. FLOODLIGHTS WARM.
EDUARDO If we were the limbs of the same body, then Mark would be the one trying to take over the body. He diluted me from 34% to less than one. He did not just leave me in the dust, he buried me. I have known Mark for a long time... Mark does not give people what they are owed. Whether it was my initial funding or the twin’s idea, Mark only uses it to soar high above us, until we become little dots from where he looks... If you believe Mark is not guilty, you support wrongdoing, injustice, and dishonor. You believe that it is right to erase people from your story. If anyone supports these things, then I am wrong, and Mark is innocent. You knew who you left behind, but you kept going anyways.
Plebeians: Murmur / commotion.
Michael: Thief!
Caleb: Liar!
JUDGE Order! Order! Court will recess. We reconvene to continue testimony.
Warm lighting for SEAN soliloquy. Lights off.
SEAN Let Eduardo have his honour. Let the twins have their verdict. My voice, like a spell, will speak the truth they tried to bury. Not through grief. Not through power. Simply by telling them what they refused to see.

SCENE 5 2:30 (15:10 total)
Location: The courtroom, continued.
Context: Eduardo has just finished testifying. Room heavy with murmurs. Mark sits with head down.
Turn on WB AND R1 lights only. Spotlight on Eduardo.
EDUARDO I did not want to shoot Mark from the high ground he believes he is in, and I do love Mark more than anybody here, but if I did not, he would only grow and grow, until he becomes a giant, and we will become ants crawling around his feet.
[Sean walks in as the audience applauds for Eduardo]
Play applause. TURN ON WB LIGHT ONLY. Spotlight on Sean.
SEAN Can I say something? (Cut applause. Nobody stops him) Eduardo Saverin is an honourable man. He gave nineteen thousand dollars. He believed in his friend. He showed up when no one else did. Honourable man. Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss are honorable men. Sons of privilege, Harvard rowers, certain from birth that the world owed them exactly what they asked for. Honourable men. And Mark Zuckerberg, according to everyone in this room tonight, is the villain of the story.
SEAN Funny. Because the thing sitting on ten million phones right now, the thing that lets you message your mother from a dorm room in California, the thing that gave a voice to people who never had one --- that came from Mark. Not from a contract. Not from nineteen thousand dollars. From one person who saw something nobody else could see and refused to stop building it.
SEAN Eduardo's shares were diluted. That's true. Was it clean? No. Was it explained the way it should have been? No. Let me ask you something. When you are building something that is growing faster than anything any of you have ever seen --- do you stop and hold a meeting? Or do you keep building? Mark kept building. Mark wanted to change the world, but Eduardo thought he would destroy it. And for that, he has been trapped and cornered by those he thought he could trust, before being slaughtered by his best friend.
TURN ON WB AND R1 LIGHT ONLY
Everyone boo and make chaos.
Michael: Lazy asshole!
Caleb: What a loser!
JUDGE Order! Order!
CAMERON (stands) He's twisting every word.
SEAN (calmly) I haven't twisted anything. I just said it out loud.
